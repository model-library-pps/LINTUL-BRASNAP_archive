**Scale of application**\
field

**Spatial resolution**\
Data collected in The Netherlands, Germany and France.

**Key outputs**\
Lintul-brasnap simulates crop growth and development, seed number and seed yield under potential growth conditions with ample supply of water and nutrients, in a disease and weed-free invironment.
The daily environmental factors are available sunlight, temperature and photoperiod during the growth period.

**Time horizon**
Growing season

**Time step of modeling**\
day
